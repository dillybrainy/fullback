# FULLBACK

## Platform

Web, Next.js App Router. Product source of truth: [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md). Current frontend behavior: [README.md](README.md) and [ACCESS-AND-PREVIEW.md](ACCESS-AND-PREVIEW.md).

## Users and purpose

FULLBACK serves selling clubs, buying clubs, academies and sell-on holders. The product explains how a transfer agreement can protect installment payments with collateral and distribute academy royalties. Federation users are read-only observers.

## Current capabilities and constraints

- Public pages are Home, Sign in, and local PDF fingerprint verification. A new browser session visits Home first.
- Workspace routes are closed until real wallet sign-in is integrated. No preview-session switch grants access.
- Wallet connection, AI reading, on-chain verification and transactions are not available in this frontend. Do not present simulated success or invent receipts.
- NEAR testnet only; fEUR is play money. Existing neutral example data and numeric shares must be preserved.
- PDF hashing happens in the browser. Static export, local image/font assets, keyboard navigation and reduced motion remain supported.
- All product-facing copy is English. Use the term Academy royalties.

## Brand commitments

FULLBACK's name, goal-frame logo, football photography and navy/yellow identity are established. The user has authorized departure from the Figma composition for the public navbar/landing improvements. Preserve product truth and access behavior while improving the presentation.
